Data Privacy Notice

MC Gadgets respects your privacy under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

What we collect

Your name and mobile number when you reserve a unit. If you apply for installment, we may also collect your income source and a photo of a valid ID.

Why

To hold your unit, contact you about it, and process your request. We do not sell your data.

Who sees it

Only authorized MC Gadgets staff and the owner. ID photos are kept in a private area, never public.

How long

Reservations expire after 24 hours. IDs of declined or withdrawn applications are deleted after 30 days.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting any MC Gadgets branch.

This notice is a draft pending the owner's and a lawyer's review.
